# Does the guidance help real people decide better? Pilot test plan

Run this only after the data checks and the back-test (`python backtest.py`) look sound.

## 1. Who and how many
- 1-2 farmer cooperatives or market-trader groups (30+ people), plus 10-15 household food buyers.
- Recruit through a trusted local partner (cooperative leader, extension officer, NGO). Written or verbal consent; no payment tied to following the advice.

## 2. Design (about 12 weeks)
- Group A uses the app. Group B keeps its usual methods (a similar group in the same season and area).
- Both groups record every sell/store/buy decision: food, quantity, date, price received or paid, spoilage loss.
- Prefer a stepped rollout (Group B gets the app later) so nobody is left out.

## 3. Measure (set success targets BEFORE starting)
| Question | Measure | Example target |
|---|---|---|
| Do they understand it? | 5-question quiz: what the signal means, how old the data is, what "estimated" means | 80% correct |
| Do they trust it appropriately? | Trust rating 1-5, and whether they check local prices before acting | Trust matches data grade |
| Do decisions improve? | Price received vs the market average that week; spoilage % | Group A better by a pre-agreed margin |
| Is it safe? | Cases where following the advice lost money; complaints | Reviewed monthly; stop rule agreed |

## 4. Safeguards
- State clearly: guidance, not a guarantee. Show data date, source and grade every time.
- Never show advice for Grade D data. Flag estimates in plain words and local languages.
- Independent review of results; publish findings, including failures.
